# E-commerce scam playbook

## How it works
A seller on a marketplace or social media asks for payment by bank transfer outside the platform and never delivers the goods. Individual losses are small but cases are numerous. Source: Singapore Police Force scam advisories.

## Indicators
A one-off payment to a new personal payee, usually small, with few coercion signals. These are the hardest scams to separate from genuine first payments.

## What to tell the customer
Pay through the platform's protected checkout. Be wary of sellers who insist on a direct bank transfer or deposit.

## Recommended response
NUDGE with a payee-confirmation prompt; avoid holding small genuine payments.
