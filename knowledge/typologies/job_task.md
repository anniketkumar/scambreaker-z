# Job and task scam playbook

## How it works
Victims are offered easy online jobs, such as liking videos or rating products, and are paid small commissions at first. They are then asked to pay in money to "unlock" bigger tasks or withdraw earnings, with the amounts rising each round. Source: Singapore Police Force scam advisories.

## Indicators
Many small payments in quick succession to new or rotating payees, often in the evening, with amounts that escalate. Sessions look normal; the customer is rarely on a call.

## What to tell the customer
A real job never asks you to pay money to earn commission or to withdraw your own earnings.

## Recommended response
NUDGE early in the series and HOLD once the pattern repeats; payees should be reviewed as possible money mules.
