# Fake friend and romance scam playbook

## How it works
A scammer builds an online relationship or impersonates a friend, then asks for money for an emergency, travel, customs fees or an investment opportunity. Source: Singapore Police Force scam advisories.

## Indicators
Payments to a recently added personal account that is not one of the customer's usual payees, often in the evening, sometimes repeated over days. Amounts are a few times the customer's usual payment.

## What to tell the customer
Someone you have only met online asking for money is a common scam. Verify the person through a call or video on a number you already know.

## Recommended response
NUDGE or HOLD depending on amount and repetition; alert the trusted contact when the customer is elderly or vulnerable.
